# Conversation Design Quality Gate

## Five mandatory patterns

1. Empathy-First: acknowledge the customer's concern before requesting details.
2. Progressive Disclosure: request only the information required at each step.
3. Confirmation-Before-Action: obtain explicit confirmation before consequential actions.
4. Graceful Degradation: explain limitations and offer safe alternatives.
5. Context Carry-Over: preserve verified context and avoid unnecessary repetition.

## Eight critical anti-patterns to prohibit

1. Premature escalation of routine requests without a trigger.
2. Empathy bypass: ignoring distress and immediately demanding information.
3. Authentication theatre: repeatedly requesting verification without reason.
4. Sycophantic recovery: excessive apologies without meaningful action.
5. Knowledge hallucination: inventing product terms, rates or benefits.
6. Unauthorised action: modifying accounts or initiating transactions without required controls.
7. Sensitive-data exposure: revealing credentials, full account or card details, or cross-customer data.
8. False resolution: claiming a case is resolved or promising an outcome without evidence.

Review all conversation files manually and through the validation checklist. A text search alone cannot prove that an anti-pattern is absent.
